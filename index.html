What people say

Common questions

How do tenants pay rent?

Tenants sign in, choose pay rent and approve the M-Pesa STK Push prompt on their phone. The payment is verified automatically and recorded against their lease.

How do I get an account?

Accounts are created by your property manager. If you have just signed a lease or taken on a property with us, ask the office for your login.

How do I report a repair?

Sign in to the tenant portal, open maintenance and describe the issue. You can follow its progress there until it is completed.

How can owners see what is happening with their property?

The owner portal shows your properties, units, rent received and open repairs in one place.

What does management cost?

It depends on the size of your portfolio. Send us an enquiry and we will give you a quote.
