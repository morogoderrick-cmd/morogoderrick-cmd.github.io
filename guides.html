For tenants

Getting your login

Your property manager creates your account when your lease starts. If you haven't received your email and password, ask the office. Then sign in from the home page.

How to pay rent by M-Pesa

  1. Sign in to your tenant portal and open the rent or payments section.
  2. Choose to pay rent and check that the M-Pesa phone number shown is the one you want to use.
  3. Confirm. A pop-up (an "STK Push") appears on your phone asking you to approve the payment.
  4. Enter your M-Pesa PIN on that pop-up only and approve.
  5. Your payment is verified automatically and recorded against your lease. You'll also get the usual M-Pesa confirmation message.
No pop-up? Check that your phone has network and that the number is registered on M-Pesa, then try again after a minute. If it still doesn't work, or money left your account but doesn't show in the portal, contact the office with your M-Pesa confirmation message.
Never share your M-Pesa PIN with anyone, including people who say they work for us. Rent is only ever requested through the pop-up on your own phone.

How to report a repair

  1. Sign in and open the maintenance section.
  2. Describe the problem, where it is, and how urgent it is.
  3. Submit it, then follow its progress until it is marked completed.

For emergencies such as a burst pipe, exposed electrical wiring or a fire risk, don't wait for the portal. Call the office straight away.

Messages and moving out

Use the built-in messages to reach management instead of scattered chats, so there's one record of what was agreed. When you plan to move out, give notice as your lease requires. Once the lease is ended, the unit is released and your account is closed out.

For owners

What you can see

Your owner portal shows each property you own, which units are occupied or vacant, rent received, and open maintenance requests, so you don't need to call the office for an update.

Getting started

  1. Tell us about your property: location, number of units, rents and current tenants. You can use the form on the home page.
  2. We agree terms and a management fee with you, based on the size of your portfolio.
  3. We add your properties and units, set up tenants and leases, and create your owner login.
  4. Rent starts coming in by M-Pesa, and you follow it all from your portal.

Renting safely in Kenya

Rental scams are common. These habits protect you wherever you look for a home.

  • See the unit in person before you pay anything. Photos can be copied from other listings.
  • Check who you are dealing with. Ask for the agent's or caretaker's ID and confirm with the property owner or management company that they act for them.
  • Be wary of rent that looks too good to be true, or anyone who rushes you to pay a "booking fee" to hold the place.
  • Pay only through channels you've verified with the office, not to a personal number someone sends you in a chat.
  • Get a written lease and a receipt for every deposit and payment, and read the lease before signing.
  • Check the basics at the viewing: water supply, power, security, and any damage you should have noted in writing.
