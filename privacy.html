Privacy policy

This policy explains how [Company legal name] ("Residia", "we", "us") handles personal data collected through this website and the Residia portal. We process personal data in line with the Data Protection Act, 2019 of Kenya.

Who we are

Residia is operated by [Company legal name], [registered address]. For anything about your data, contact us at [privacy email address] or [phone number].

What we collect

  • Enquiry forms: your name, phone number, email (if you give it) and the message you send. Vacancy alert requests also include your preferred bedrooms and rent.
  • Portal accounts: your email and password (stored in hashed form), and, for tenants and owners, lease details, rent payment records, maintenance requests and messages sent through the portal.
  • M-Pesa payments: the phone number used and transaction details returned by Safaricom. We never see your M-Pesa PIN.
  • On your device: this site stores a sign-in token, your language choice and any units you save in your browser's local storage. These stay on your device and you can clear them at any time.
  • Usage statistics: [if analytics is on: we use Plausible Analytics, which counts visits without cookies and without tracking you across sites. Otherwise delete this line.]

Why we use it

  • To answer your enquiries and arrange viewings.
  • To create and run your tenant, owner or manager account, collect and record rent, and handle repairs.
  • To contact you about a vacancy you asked to hear about.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse such as spam.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

Our lawful bases are your consent (for example when you send an enquiry or ask for a vacancy alert), performance of a contract (your tenancy or management agreement), legal obligation, and our legitimate interest in running and securing the service.

Who we share it with

  • The property owner or manager responsible for the unit you enquire about or rent.
  • Safaricom (M-Pesa) to process rent payments.
  • Service providers who host our systems or send notifications for us: [hosting provider, email/SMS provider].
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

We do not sell personal data. [If you store or process data outside Kenya, say so here and name the safeguards you use.]

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept for [for example, 12 months] unless they lead to a tenancy or management agreement. Tenancy, payment and maintenance records are kept for [for example, 7 years] after the agreement ends to meet accounting and legal needs. After that we delete or anonymise them.

Your rights

You can ask us to tell you what personal data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop using it for a particular purpose, and you can withdraw consent at any time. Email [privacy email address] and we will respond within the time the law allows. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (odpc.go.ke).

Security

We use encrypted connections, hashed passwords and access controls so each person only sees the data that belongs to them. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data we will tell you and the regulator as the law requires.

Children

This website is not meant for people under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. For significant changes affecting portal users we will tell you directly.

Website terms

  • Listings. Vacancies shown here are meant to be accurate, but availability, rent and details can change. A listing is not an offer; a tenancy only exists once you have signed a lease with us or the owner.
  • Sample listings. If you see a "Sample listing" label, that unit is an example and cannot be rented.
  • Accounts. Accounts are created by your property manager. Keep your password private and tell us straight away if you think someone else has used your account.
  • Payments. Rent paid through M-Pesa is recorded once Safaricom confirms it. Contact us if a payment does not appear within [for example, 24 hours].
  • Acceptable use. Do not misuse the site: no spam, no attempts to break into accounts or systems, and no false enquiries.
  • Liability. We take care to keep the site running and accurate, but we provide it "as is" and are not responsible for losses caused by things outside our control, such as network or M-Pesa outages. Nothing here limits liability that cannot be limited by law.
  • Governing law. These terms are governed by the laws of Kenya, and Kenyan courts have jurisdiction.
